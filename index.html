04 · Before anything else

Problem 1. Your Google reviews need cleaning up, and complaints need real replies.

1
reply, pasted everywhere
the same line under 5-star praise and 1-star complaints
3
recent complaints, no reply
two of them about loans reported wrongly to CIBIL
"Your kind words and constructive feedback inspire us to improve continually."Owner reply under a review that said "Very bad customer service".
What we found

A few recent 5-star reviews appear to come from people inside the company. Complaints get a template reply, or none.

What it costs you

Google removes reviews from staff when it spots them. A borrower who notices stops trusting the other 5-star reviews too. An open CIBIL complaint keeps ranking for years.

The fix

Remove staff reviews. Ask every customer for a review after disbursal. Reply to every review within 48 hours in your own words. Send CIBIL complaints to the grievance officer, publicly and politely.
