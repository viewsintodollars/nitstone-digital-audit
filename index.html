07 · Website

Problem 6. A gold loan is an emergency, and there's no number to call.

People take a gold loan when something has gone wrong

A hospital bill, a lost job, a gap in the business. From our work with borrowers, gold loans are the fastest-growing loan in India, and a sign of stress on a credit report. Nobody is browsing. They need money today.

The homepage has no phone number and no WhatsApp

Checked on a phone and a laptop on 22 September: no number, no call link, no WhatsApp link on the homepage. The only button is a form. Branch numbers are on a separate Branches page, and the contact page shows one landline.

RBI number not shown

It's the first thing a careful borrower checks. It belongs in the footer of every page.

What we found

Someone in an emergency lands on the homepage and has to find the Branches page, or fill a form and wait.

What it costs you

In an emergency, people call the first lender who answers. The next lender's number is one search away.

The fix

A call button and a WhatsApp button fixed to the bottom of every page, going to the nearest branch. Branches with maps. RBI number in the footer.

11 · Reddit and Quora

How we do it for Expert Panel without getting banned.

We run Reddit and Quora end to end for Expert Panel, a debt-resolution brand working under the same kind of rules you do. The account belongs to a named advisor, not the brand.

01

A person, not a logo

An advisor profile with the company link disclosed in the bio. Reddit and Quora trust people. They ban brand handles that only promote.

02

9 in 10 answers carry no link

The answer solves the problem on its own. At most one in ten mentions the brand, and only where the forum allows it.

03

Sources, not sales

Every answer cites RBI or government sources. For Expert Panel, cited answers did better than anything else we posted.

04

Earn trust first

A new account spends its first 30 days only helping. No links, no DMs, no second accounts, no vote rings.

For Nitstone: answer the gold loan questions people already ask, and let the bio do the selling.

15 · Compliance

In lending, every post is a regulated ad. We build the checks in first.

Claims

Only say what you can prove

"One of the top finance companies in India" is on your bio and site title. Advertising rules ask you to back a claim like that, or drop it. "Gold loan in 30 minutes" is fine if every branch can deliver it.

Rates

Show the full cost, clearly

RBI requires a Key Facts Statement with the all-in annual rate before a loan is signed. Any rate in an ad should match it and never read cheaper than it is.

Trust signals

RBI number and grievance officer

Registration number on every page, and the grievance officer's name and contact where borrowers can find them, as RBI's Fair Practices Code expects.

Ads

Meta treats loans as a special category

Loan ads get limited age, gender and postcode targeting on Meta. Campaigns are planned around branch radius and content instead. We confirm current rules in Ads Manager before launch.

How we run it: topics and scripts approved before filming, one written checklist, nothing goes live without sign-off.
